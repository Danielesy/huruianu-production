01 — The beginning

A two-minute idea

It started as a dare. In 2023, a group of students from the Republic of Moldova sat down with a rough idea, a minimal setup, and zero expectations — just the shared feeling that making something real would be worth a try.

"We thought we'd film for an afternoon. We ended up finding our calling."

The result was a 2-minute short with a proper scenario and real actors. The moment playback ended, something clicked. The energy on set, the storytelling, the craft of it — it felt like something none of them were ready to walk away from.

02 — First film

From a spark to a full feature

What followed was months of writing. A full screenplay took shape — bigger characters, a longer arc, a real story to tell. By 2024, Huruianu Production had its first short film: Fulla Net, running 25 minutes.

The production pushed the team to invest in their craft. New cameras, lighting rigs and audio gear were brought in — tools that didn't just improve the image, but expanded what was possible to imagine. Each piece of kit was a commitment to the next project, and the one after that.

03 — Building momentum

Keeping the fire going

Between major productions, the team stayed sharp with music videos, creative commissions and university projects. Each one was a proving ground — a chance to try new techniques, build relationships and refine the visual language that makes Huruianu Production recognisable.

The smaller projects weren't filler. They were the training ground that made the next big thing possible.

04 — Latest work

Mihail Bogomutov

On 15 January 2026, Huruianu Production released its most ambitious work to date: the short film Mihail Bogomutov. A project years in the making, brought to life by a team that had grown — technically, creatively, and as filmmakers — through every frame shot before it.

The story doesn't stop here. It's just getting started.
