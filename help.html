How it works
Working with us

From idea to screen

01
Reach out
Contact us via the Contact page, email or phone. Tell us about your idea, project type and timeline — the more detail the better.
02
We discuss
We'll schedule a call or meeting to understand your vision, goals and budget. Together we shape the concept and define what the final result should look and feel like.
03
We produce
Our team handles everything — scripting, casting, shooting and post-production. We keep you updated throughout the process so there are no surprises.
04
You receive
The final video is delivered in your preferred format and published wherever you need it. We're available for revisions to make sure you're fully satisfied.
Site guide
Navigating the site

How to use this website

🏠
Home page
The Home page is your starting point. Scroll past the hero video to browse our featured projects — hover over any card to see details, then click to watch it on YouTube. Continue scrolling to explore our backstage gallery.
🖼
Backstage gallery
On the Home page, scroll down to the Backstage section. Click any photo to open it in fullscreen. Use the Prev / Next buttons or your keyboard ← → arrow keys to navigate between photos. Press Esc to close.
👥
About page
The About page tells the story of Huruianu Production from 2023 to today, with a timeline on the left. Scroll down to meet the crew — click any team photo to view it fullscreen.
✉
Contact page
The Contact page lets you reach us directly — click the phone or email cards to open your dialer or mail client instantly. You can also fill in the message form to send us a project inquiry.
📱
Mobile navigation
On phones and tablets the top navigation collapses into a ☰ menu button in the top right corner. Tap it to open the full-screen menu, tap any link to navigate, or tap the ✕ to close it.
▶
Watching our films
All project cards on the Home page link directly to YouTube. They open in a new tab so you never lose your place on the site. You can also visit our channel directly at @danielhuruianu.
